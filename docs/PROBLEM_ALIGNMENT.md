# Problem statement alignment

The challenge, sentence by sentence, and where AirWise answers it.

| Challenge requirement                                        | What AirWise does                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Code                                                                          | Test                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| "A GenAI-powered assistant"                                  | Gemini on Vertex AI (no key) or the Gemini API explains today's situation in English, Hindi or Marathi and answers questions grounded in the family's numbers; a rule-based assistant answers when the AI is unavailable | `server/ai.ts`, `shared/ask.ts`                                               | `server/ai.test.ts`, `shared/ask.test.ts`, e2e        |
| "…helps **families and communities**…"                       | A household form built around babies, children, older adults and asthma; community actions (ask the school to move sports indoors, share with neighbours); one-tap share for WhatsApp                                    | `client/components/HouseholdForm.tsx`, `shared/catalog.ts`, `shared/share.ts` | `client/App.test.tsx`, `shared/share.test.ts`         |
| "…navigate **severe winter smog and urban air pollution**…"  | India's CPCB AQI categories up to Severe, the family level (vulnerable members raise it), live indicative AQI for 21 cities, and the cold-air logic behind dawn and evening peaks                                        | `shared/aqi.ts`, `shared/risk.ts`, `shared/daily.ts`                          | `shared/aqi.test.ts`, `shared/risk.test.ts`           |
| "**Low-emission daily routines**"                            | A four-part day plan tailored to the level, children, exercise and commute, plus the **best and worst two hours outdoors today** from the live forecast                                                                  | `shared/daily.ts`, `shared/windows.ts`                                        | `shared/daily.test.ts`, `shared/windows.test.ts`, e2e |
| "**Indoor air quality hacks**"                               | HEPA purifier, DIY Corsi–Rosenthal box with build steps, window timing, a clean-air room, cooking ventilation, damp-mopping, and a **room-by-room CADR check** (4 air changes an hour, minutes to clear 90%)             | `shared/catalog.ts`, `shared/iaq.ts`, `client/components/Indoor.tsx`          | `shared/iaq.test.ts`, e2e                             |
| "**Sustainable alternatives**"                               | Two-wheeler or car to metro or shared rides with kg CO₂e per week, induction or LPG instead of biomass, no open burning, and a **commute comparison table** (time, PM2.5 breathed in, CO₂e) for every mode               | `shared/catalog.ts`, `shared/commute.ts`, `client/components/Commute.tsx`     | `shared/commute.test.ts`, e2e                         |
| "…**tailored to their location**…"                           | City selection or **"Use my location"** (matched to the nearest listed city on the device, coordinates never sent); the live hourly forecast for that city                                                               | `shared/cities.ts`, `client/locate.ts`, `server/aqiProvider.ts`               | `e2e/app.spec.ts` (no coordinates in any request)     |
| "…and **lifestyle**"                                         | Commute mode and length, children's outdoor time, exercise, mask habit, cooking fuel, windows, purifier                                                                                                                  | `shared/household.ts`, `shared/exposure.ts`                                   | `shared/exposure.test.ts`                             |
| "…**clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices**" | Every action shows its measured **% drop in the family's daily PM2.5 intake**, an effort level, an indicative ₹ cost and a plain-language why; bars compare today as lived with the top steps                            | `shared/actions.ts`, `client/components/Results.tsx`                          | `shared/actions.test.ts`, `client/App.test.tsx`       |
| "Smart, dynamic assistant"                                   | The whole plan recomputes on every change; the assistant answers in context                                                                                                                                              | `client/useAssistant.ts`                                                      | `client/App.test.tsx`                                 |
